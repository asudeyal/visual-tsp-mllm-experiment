Use this priority order:

1. CAPACITY AND VEHICLE FEASIBILITY
- Prefer candidates in which every route appears to stay within the visual full-capacity reference.
- Use the visual demand markers to judge whether customers assigned to the same vehicle visually fit together.
- If vehicle-availability markers are visible, prefer candidates that do not appear to use more vehicles than available.

2. CUSTOMER COVERAGE AND ROUTE STRUCTURE
- Prefer candidates that appear to visit every visible customer exactly once.
- Prefer candidates whose routes start and end at the depot.
- Penalize visible omissions, duplicated visits, disconnected route fragments, empty routes, or suspicious depot usage.

3. ROUTE GEOMETRY
- Prefer fewer crossings.
- Prefer less backtracking and line overlap.
- Prefer fewer unnecessary zigzags.
- Prefer fewer visually obvious detours and long-looking jumps.

4. GEOGRAPHIC COHERENCE
- Prefer customer clusters that are visually compact and locally continuous.
- Prefer routes with smooth regional flow rather than abrupt direction changes.
